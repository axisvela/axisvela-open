# ORCHADYN

**Repository:** [axisvela/orchadyn-open](https://github.com/axisvela/orchadyn-open) ·
**License:** Apache-2.0 · **Language:** Go · **Layer:** Work and capabilities

> A planning compiler that turns goals, state, capabilities, and constraints
> into verified, revisable plans.

## The problem

Plans generated by a model are hard to inspect, compare, or revise. They mix
what should happen with how it will happen, ignore capability boundaries, and
lose the constraints that made them valid in the first place.

Orchadyn treats planning as compilation: inputs are explicit, the output is a
structured plan, and the plan can be verified and revised.

## What it provides

- A runtime-neutral planning model.
- Plan generation from goals, state, capabilities, and constraints.
- Plan verification and explanation.
- Plan revision when assumptions change.
- HTTP, MCP, and CLI interfaces.
- A plan ledger for traceability.

## Why it matters

- **Explainable plans.** A plan is an artifact that can be reviewed, not a
  paragraph in a chat.
- **Revisable.** When the world changes, the plan can be revised without
  starting over.
- **Governed.** Plans are constrained by the capabilities and policies that will
  execute them.

## How it fits

Orchadyn sits between intent and execution. Its plans can be governed by
[MODUREGIS](moduregis.md), executed by [RHEOVELA](rheovela.md), reviewed by
[SYMBIVELA](symbivela.md), and verified by [TEKMOVELA](tekmovela.md).
