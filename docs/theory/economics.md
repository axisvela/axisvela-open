# Agent economics: a lens, not a forecast

**Sources:** Huiwen Han, *The Inference Rent* (SSRN, 2026),
[doi:10.2139/ssrn.6582419](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6582419); *Competing in
the Post-Code Era* (2026),
[doi:10.5281/zenodo.21550394](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21550394); *The
Enclosure of Episteme* (2026),
[doi:10.5281/zenodo.21551616](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21551616).

> When execution becomes probabilistic and replication becomes cheap, the
> economics of software change: what is scarce moves, and so does the basis of
> durable advantage.

This page is a short orientation to a research program on the political economy
of cognitive infrastructure. It is **not** investment, procurement, or legal
advice. The papers are working papers and preprints; check their status before
relying on specific claims.

## Three ideas worth knowing

### Inference rent

If model inference is a metered input to production, then platform owners can
capture rent at the inference layer. The *Inference Rent* paper studies
manufactured scarcity, physical constraints, and the durability of platform rent
in the foundation-model era.

For adopters, the practical questions are:

- Which costs scale with usage, and which are fixed?
- What is the exit cost if a provider changes price or policy?
- Can routing, caching, or local models reduce dependence?

AxisVela relevance: [NOETIVELA](../projects/noetivela.md) makes model and
endpoint routing explicit, which is a prerequisite for cost and sovereignty
control.

### Exposure and replication

The Exposure–Replication model argues that assets which are highly observable
and fully articulable become replicable once model capability crosses an
asset-specific threshold. Durable advantage then migrates toward assets that are
tacit, hidden, irreversibly experiential, or networked.

For agent builders, this suggests:

- code logic alone is a weak moat;
- proprietary context, relationships, and operational evidence matter more;
- validation and trust can become a premium.

AxisVela relevance: [GNOSIVELA](../projects/gnosivela.md),
[ONTOVELA](../projects/ontovela.md), and
[TEKMOVELA](../projects/tekmovela.md) invest in context, evidence, and
verification rather than only in generated code.

### Validation premium

As generated outputs proliferate, the scarce good becomes trustworthy
validation: knowing which output is correct, safe, and fit for a decision. This
is one reason the ecosystem treats verification and evidence as products, not as
test scripts.

## Build vs buy, through this lens

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Is the capability a commodity or a differentiator? | Commodity capabilities are candidates for routing or buying. |
| Where does the context live? | Context and relationships are harder to replicate than code. |
| Who owns the evidence? | Evidence is the basis for audit, learning, and trust. |
| What is the exit path? | Contracts and open surfaces reduce lock-in. |
| What is the cost curve? | Inference-heavy designs have different economics from deterministic ones. |

## A caution

Economics arguments are easy to over-extend. The cited works make specific
claims under specific models; this page only extracts design questions. Use the
original papers for the actual arguments and evidence.
