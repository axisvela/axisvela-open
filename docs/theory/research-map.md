## Other works referenced in the profile

The author’s broader program includes *The AI Trilemma*, *Signalling Inflation
and Rational Adaptation*, *Semantic Entropy and Structural Invariance*, *The
Expansion–Compression Loop*, *Mean Reversion or Innovation Collapse?*,
*Cyber-Grooming*, *Prompt ≠ Knowledge*, and several mathematical notes. They are
not central to the engineering guidance here, but they form the wider research
context. See the author profiles for the current list and links.

## How to cite this documentation

If you cite the synthesis, cite this repository and the relevant source paper.
Do not cite the synthesis as if it were the original theoretical work.
